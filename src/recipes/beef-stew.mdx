<Recipe>
1. Cut **3 lb beef chuck roast into 2-inch cubes**.
1. Preheat a skillet with **high heat cooking oil**. Add beef in a single layer brown on both sides.[^1] Add to slow cooker.
2. In the same skillet, saute **1 large diced yellow onion** until lightly browned, adding more oil if needed. Add to slow cooker.
3. To slow cooker, add **4 large carrots, cut into bite size pieces**, **1 lb Yukon potatoes, cut into bite size pieces**, **1/3 cup apple cider juice**[^2], **3 tbsp tomato paste**, **3 cups beef broth**, **1 tsp ground black pepper**, **1 tbsp fine sea salt** (or added to taste), and **2 bay leaves**.
4. Cook on high 4 to 5 hours or on low for 7 to 9 hours.
4. Remove bay leaves, season to taste, and serve garnished with **finely chopped parsley**.
</Recipe>

<Notes>
Original recipe by [Natasha's Kitchen](https://natashaskitchen.com/slow-cooker-beef-stew/).
</Notes>

<Footnotes>
[^1]: Don't crowd the pan and cook beef in batches if needed.
[^2]: NOT vinegar.
</Footnotes>
